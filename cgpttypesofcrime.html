Types of Cybercrime

Phishing

What is it?
Fake emails/messages used to steal passwords, bank details, or personal info.

Government Steps:
• CERT-IN alerts
• Blocking fraudulent websites
• Awareness programs through Digital India

What citizens can do:
• Never click unknown links
• Check sender email
• Enable 2FA

Identity Theft

What is it?
Criminals steal your Aadhaar, PAN, or bank identity to misuse it.

Government Steps:
• Strict KYC rules
• Cyber Financial Crime reporting portal

What citizens can do:
• Do not share OTP
• Keep documents private

Online Shopping / UPI Fraud

What is it?
Fake sellers, fake payment links, or UPI scam calls.

Government Steps:
• 1930 Cyber Fraud helpline
• Razorpay/PhonePe fraud detection

What citizens can do:
• Only buy from trusted sites
• Double check QR codes

Cyberbullying & Harassment

What is it?
Online threats, blackmail, abuse, or humiliation.

Government Steps:
• IPC 354D, IT Act Section 66E
• Women Cyber Cell support

What citizens can do:
• Block & report
• Keep screenshots as evidence

Hacking

What is it?
Unauthorized access to computers, networks, or accounts.

Government Steps:
• National Cyber Coordination Centre (NCCC)
• CERT-IN monitoring

What citizens can do:
• Strong passwords
• Update software

Data Breach

What is it?
Personal or company data leaked online.

Government Steps:
• New data protection bill (DPDP Act)
• Mandatory breach reporting

What citizens can do:
• Avoid giving data to shady apps

Social Engineering

What is it?
Tricks used to fool people into revealing secrets.

What citizens can do:
• Think before trusting unknown people online

Malware / Virus Attacks

What is it?
Harmful software that steals or deletes data.

Ransomware

What is it?
Hackers lock your files and demand money.

Cyberstalking

What is it?
Repeated online monitoring of someone's activities.
